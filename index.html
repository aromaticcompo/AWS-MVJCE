03 / A few good questions

Start where
you are.

No cloud background required. Bring curiosity, follow a question, and take the next step at your pace.

What is AWS@MVJCE?
A student-led community at MVJCE focused on exploring cloud technology through learning, building, and sharing.
Do I need cloud experience before I start?
No. Begin with the fundamentals, ask questions as you go, and build your understanding one concept at a time.
How can I start learning?
Explore the AWS training resources linked here, choose a topic that interests you, and try a small experiment you can explain.
What does “building together” mean?
Learn in the open: share what you tried, document what worked, and make it easier for someone else to continue from there.
