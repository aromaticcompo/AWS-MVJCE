CONCEPT PROPOSAL · FOR TEAM DISCUSSION01 / 05
01 / Visual direction

Clean by default.
Distinctive in motion.

A warm-white canvas, charcoal typography, restrained AWS-inspired orange, and a single server-rack illustration. Thin circuit pathways provide the signature detail without competing with the content.

The goal is to feel considered and technically literate—not like a template packed with effects.

Design principles

Less noise.
More intent.

Warm white · Charcoal · Orange

AWS@MVJCE · DESIGN DIRECTION02 / 05
02 / Signature animations

One memorable entrance.
Four refined details.

Recommended motion system: quick, quiet, and always in service of the content.

01 / INTRO

Server boot-up

Rack lights activate, a short initialization status advances, then the homepage appears. Skippable and brief.

02 / HERO

Living data pathways

Small orange pulses travel through the circuit lines. Keep the headline still and easy to read.

03 / SCROLL

Editorial reveals

Sections fade and rise a few pixels as they enter view. No forced scrolling or long delays.

04 / HOVER

Precision interactions

Event arrows shift slightly, project cards lift subtly, and fine orange accents appear on hover.

05 / OPTIONAL

Closing connection

A thin circuit line leads toward the Join button, giving the page a coherent final note.

GUARDRAILS

Performance first

Support reduced motion, avoid heavy video backgrounds, and keep mobile interactions lightweight.
