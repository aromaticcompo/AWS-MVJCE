01 / ORIGIN

A place to start from zero.

AWSSBG is the AWS Student Builder Group at MVJCE. We learn cloud fundamentals, join hands-on sessions, and explore what we can make together.

Curiosity is a good qualification.

You do not need a finished project or a list of cloud certifications. Start with a question and take the next step through a session or shared build.

The climb is shared.

Try a tool, explain what you learned, ask for another perspective. The community grows through those small exchanges.
